PanScopus Browser Companion

Automatic Scopus search → full CSV selection → download → import to PanScopus Cloud, through your normally authorized Chrome session. No Scopus API or local server.

One-time setup: choose the folder where Chrome saves downloaded files (normally Downloads). Companion uses that folder only to read the new Scopus CSV for this run.

Not selected

Keep your authorized Scopus session signed in. Do not share institutional credentials with the extension.
